# Version 3: the professions' new purpose, and cosmetics

Gear comes from the dungeons, so the three professions (mining, woodcutting, gathering) pay in other ways. Tests: `node tools/revamp-smoke.js`.

## Your home
A private plot (`HOME_YARD`, a clearing near the home village found by `shared/professions.js`, radius `HOME_PLOT_R`). You enter with `home{a:'enter'}` (the *Go home* button of any Lodge panel; `homeP`, `server/professions.js`) and leave with `home{a:'leave'}` (the bench's Lodge panel); the position you left from is `p.homeFrom`. While `homeAt(x,z)` you are alone: `homeSnapS` (hook in `broadcastSnap`) cuts other players out of your snapshots and you out of theirs, `nearestFighter` skips you, and the props (workbench, fence) and the nodes are drawn only to someone at home. The bench (talk key) opens the Lodge panel: cosmetics are made and resources sold there too. 18 nodes: a vein of each of the six ore grades, a tree of each log grade, each of the six herbs; the tool tier you wear decides how far up you can work. They are the **last** 18 entries of `NODES` (`home:true`), so older node numbers do not move.
The nodes are **per player**: `p.hback` (not saved) holds when each comes back (`HOME_RESPAWN`, 60 s) and node events carry `e.to`, so nobody else sees it taken. Server: `gatherOkP`, `finishGatherP`, `homeRewardP`, `updateNodesS` (`server/professions.js`).
Rewards of a gather there besides the material, small on purpose: exp (`HOME_XP` of the level's exp times the grade), coins (`HOME_COIN` of a kill's), and rarely (`HOME_UPG` times the grade) an upgrade item: a Tempering Stone from level 30, a skill-upgrade material below.

## Tools' small bonus
A tool worn in its slot of tier 3 or better (Silverstone) adds attack (pickaxe), health (axe) or defence (sickle): `TOOL_BONUS_*`, `toolBonusOf` in `shared/professions.js`, read in `recalcP`. At most 2% each (rarity adds a little), 6% in all.

## Potions
`regen` (Mossblood Draught: heals a share of your health every second for 20 s), `scholar` (+exp, 5 min), `fortune` (+coins and drop chances, 5 min), each in three strengths, keys `pot4`..`pot6` (T, U, O). `POT_KINDS`, `potVal`, `potDur` (`shared/crafting.js`), `updatePotionsS` (`server/crafting.js`), `rewardKill` reads `scholar` and `fortune`.

## Auras and capes
`shared/cosmetics.js` (7 auras, 7 capes, costs in ore or logs + a herb + coins, `gear.cos`), `server/cosmetics.js` (`cosmake`), `cosCleanLook` (in `newPlayer` and the `look` message: no unowned aura or cape), the model `game/character/cosmetics.js` (`cosDress`, called at the end of `buildCharacter`; loose meshes moved by `rig.anims`), the lodge's Cosmetics list `game/economy/cosmetics.js`, the editor's Cosmetics tab. Look fields `aura` and `cape` (default `''`). See one: `node tools/model-preview.js out.png --looks '[{"cape":"regal","aura":"stellar"}]'`.

## Version 3.2: the general pieces, crafting and try-on
Seven slots hold pieces that belong to no set: hat, top, bottom, shoes, gloves, cape, aura (`CP_SLOTS` in `shared/cospieces.js`, the letters h/t/b/s/g/c/a are the first letter of a `gear.cos` key such as `'h:catphones'`). Every base garment from before is **free**; new pieces (`CP_PIECES`) and most capes and auras are **crafted** at a Wayfarers' Lodge from profession resources (`gear.res`) and/or monster drops (`gear.mats`) plus coins (`CP_RECIPE`, the materials fit the piece: ore for metal, logs for wood, herbs for cloth, a matching monster's drop for hide, scales and the like). Free capes: Backpack (the old `pack` switch is now `cape:'pack'`, migrated in `fullLook`), Wayfarer's, Mossback; free auras: Firefly, Dewfall.
- Server: `cosMakeP` (`server/cosmetics.js`) refuses free pieces and takes materials; `cosCleanLook` strips any crafted piece you do not own, in every slot.
- Colours: `look.capeColor` / `capeTrim` / `auraColor` / `auraColor2` (-1 = as designed), read through `cosTint` (`game/character/cosmetics.js`).
- Eyes: `look.pupil` (`FACE_PUPILS`), `look.eyes2` (second eye colour), `EYEC_MORE`. Chest slider max 2.2, in the Shape group, widened ribs through `chestEff` / `chestRibs`.
- Bold garments (crop, halter, harness, loincloth, short shorts...) draw underwear where skin would otherwise show (`gmxBare*`, `model-garments-more.js`); the new hats are in `model-hats-more.js`, the new gloves in `GL_BASE`.
- **Try-on**: the editor lists locked pieces with a padlock and their recipe and lets you wear them; while a locked piece is worn (`edTrying`) the look is neither saved nor sent (`saveLook`, `lookForNet(LOOK_SAVED)`) and the Done button is blocked until every worn part is owned or you go back (`edRevert`).
Test: `node tools/cospieces-smoke.js`.

### Decorations (slot `d`)
Small details worn **up to three at once**, in any of `look.deco1`, `deco2`, `deco3` (a decoration does not belong to a place: the same piece may sit in any field), each with its own colour `decoColor1..3` (-1 = as designed). All 19 are crafted (none is free): `CP_PIECES` rows with slot `d` and recipes `'d:<id>'` in `shared/cospieces.js`. `DECOS` (`game/character/model-decos.js`, `decoReg(id, anchor, builder)`) draws each as a small merged mesh on a rig anchor (`headTop`, `face`, `neck`, `chest`, `back`, `waist`, `shoulderR`, `wristL`, `ankleL`), a few with a motion in `rig.anims` (halo and moth bob, the tail sways). A new decoration is one `decoReg`, a `DECO_COL` colour, a `CP_PIECES` row and a recipe. The editor's Decor tab has three rows with a colour picker each; `cosLocked` returns the `field` of each locked decoration so `cosCleanLook` strips exactly that field.

### Multi-colour crafted tops and bottoms
Like a set's piece, every crafted top and bottom (`GMX_TOPS` / `GMX_BOTTOMS`) has up to four colours: the main one (`topColor` / `bottomColor`) and `tintTop2..4` / `tintBottom2..4` (-1 = as designed). `GMX_PAL` (`model-garments-more.js`) lists each piece's design colours; `gmxPalAt(L, slot)` resolves them for the builders and `gmxPalDef` feeds the editor's "Second / Third / Fourth colour" pickers. Free base garments stay single-colour. Test: `cospieces-smoke`.

### Shields
An optional cosmetic worn on the left forearm, off by default (`look.shield = ''`): four shapes (Buckler, Heater, Kite, Tower Shield), crafted like the rest (slot `w`, recipes `'w:<id>'`), colours `shieldColor` (field) and `shieldTrim` (rim, boss, emblem). `SHIELDS` / `shieldDress` in `game/character/model-shields.js`; it changes no number and no weapon. The editor's Shield tab lists them.

### Child-safe setting
Settings > Child-safe (off by default, the page reloads to apply it; stored in `localStorage` as `wildwood-modest`). When on, `buildCharacter` first passes every look through `modestLook` (`game/character/modest.js`), so every person this client draws, other players too, wears the plain stand-in for each bare crafted piece (crop, halter, harness become the tunic; loincloth, short shorts and wrap skirt become trousers), and the five signature sets that bare skin (Sabir's chest and arms, Genzo's right side, Nephra's midriff, Marigold's midriff and short skirt, Torgeir's left arm) ask `modestOn()` and draw their covered version. Nothing is sent or saved. Test: `cospieces-smoke`.

### Switching the sex of a worn set
Signature sets are designed for one gender (their reference setup in `CHAR_MODELS.<id>.setups`; keep designing them that way). When the player flips the Gender row while a set is worn, `charSexFit` (`shared/character.js`) moves the sliders to the new sex's reference setup (the set's own if it has one, else the base model's), keeps the height and clamps every slider into the set's recommended ranges, so the pieces still fit the body without changing the design. Test: `cospieces-smoke`.

### Silly hand items and left-handedness (version 3.3)
Eighteen crafted cosmetics held in a hand, for looks only (slots `v` strong hand, `u` weak hand in `shared/cospieces.js`; look fields `strongItem` / `weakItem` with `strongColor` / `strongTrim` / `weakColor` / `weakTrim`, -1 = as designed). A strong-hand item (frying pan, rubber chicken, giant trout, baguette, squeaky mallet, giant carrot, giant lollipop, umbrella, rolling pin, fly swatter) **replaces the weapon you carry** in the picture (`weaponsOn` returns nothing, `rig.sillyS`) and a weak-hand item (pot lid, dinner tray, rubber duck, pillow, teddy bear, whole pizza, giant cookie, ice-cream cone) replaces the shield, and the archer's bow (`rig.sillyW`); the real weapon still fights. Models: `SILLY` / `sillyDress` in `game/character/model-silly.js` (a builder gets the two colours and returns parts: strong items point down from the hand like the sword, weak items are drawn like a shield). Editor tabs "Strong hand" and "Weak hand", the Lodge lists them, `cosCleanLook` strips unmade ones. Test: `node tools/silly-smoke.js`; see them: `node tools/model-preview.js out.png --hold --front --size 380 560 --looks '[{"strongItem":"pan","weakItem":"potlid"}]'`.
**Left-handed** (Settings > Handedness, default right): `look.left` (saved with the look, so others see it). `buildCharacter` sets `rig.left`; `weaponsOn` puts the sword / wand in the left hand, the bow's hand and the quiver swap, `shieldDress` and `sillyDress` use the right forearm; `poseRig` (`character/pose.js`) mirrors the arms, the attack twist and the holds when `rig.left`.

### Walking and swaying cloth
`poseRig` (`character/pose.js`, used for you, other players, villagers and folk monsters) walks with a two-bend knee (a small give at the heel strike, a big fold as the leg swings through), the pelvis turning, rolling and sliding over the standing leg, shoulders turning against it, elbows folding as the arm comes forward and the head kept level; a standing hiker shifts weight. Skirts, tunic flares and a set's hip cloth (meshes in the hips' frame) sway through `clSway` (`character/cloth.js`, pushed on `rig.anims`): every vertex below the waist is pushed out of each leg's way, trails behind with speed and ripples; nothing moves when standing still. Preview a stride: `node tools/model-preview.js out.png --walk`.

### Colours: the picker, favourites and premium colours (version 3.3)
Every colour in the editor is one **colour block** (`edColourBlock`, `ui/colour-picker.js`), named "Colour 1", "Colour 2"... in the order the piece uses them (a cape: 1 cloth, 2 trim; an aura: 1 main, 2 second; a worn set's piece: its further colours; the hair: 1 hair, 2 gradient end, 3 third), each with an optional Designed button, **your 16 favourites** (`gear.fav`, saved on the account, starts as the 16 free colours of `TINTC`; "Organise favourites" turns on tap-to-add, drag or arrows to reorder) and an "All colours" drop-down with the row's free colours and the **48 premium colours** (`PREM_COLOURS`, `shared/colours.js`). Rows opt in or out with `premium:false` (the skin and the Kin fur have none) and carry `num` / `hint` (`EDIT`, `ui/character-editor.js`); the old three renderers (`color`, `dcolor`, `palette`) all call the one block.
A premium colour costs `PREM_PRICE` = 500 gems **once for the whole account** (`gear.pc`, message `colbuy{i}`, `server/colours.js`) and then works in every colour row of every piece. Anyone may try one on in the editor; wearing one you do not own is a try-on like an unmade crafted piece (`premLocked` joins `edLocks`: not saved, not sent, Done blocked, the bar offers Buy), and the server puts such a field back (`premCleanLook`, called next to `cosCleanLook` in `newPlayer` and the `look` message: a base colour goes to a plain one, every other colour field to -1 = as designed). The public test world (`/test`) owns all 48 (`server/testworld.js`). Favourites: `colfav{l}` (`premSanitize` cleans: whole numbers, no repeats, at most 16). Test: `node tools/colours-smoke.js`.

### Dresses and curved skirts (version 3.3)
Six crafted dresses are tops (slot `t`: sundress, peasant, tiered, mermaid, ballgown, wrapdress) that carry their own skirt: `game/character/model-dresses.js` (`GMD` numbers per dress, `gmdTop` the bodice and straps, `gmdHips` the skirt, `gmdPaint` the torso paint). A dress ignores the bottom piece (`gmxAliasLook`). The skirt is a ring loft with a hip swell, folds that deepen to the hem and a waved hem; each colour edge has two rows a hair apart (`GMD_EDGES`) so the line is crisp. Colours: `gmxPalAt(L,'top')`, 3-4 per dress, every one used (`cospieces-smoke` checks). Walking: `clSway(rig,mesh,loose)` in `cloth.js`; a dress passes `loose`, so the skirt is pushed ahead of a forward leg and behind a back one as a smooth bump (the base skirt keeps the squeeze-round-the-leg circles). The base skirt, kilt, hakama, wrap skirt and the tunic's flare were reshaped too (hip swell, soft folds, waved hem). Modest mode swaps the bare-shouldered mermaid and ballgown (`MODEST_TOP`). Test: `node tools/dresses-smoke.js`; see them: `node tools/model-preview.js out.png --front --looks '[{"sex":"female","top":"ballgown"}]'` (add `--walk`).
