# The protocol: every message and event

Moved from CLAUDE.md section 3 (the router keeps the shape of it and the recipe for a new message). The code: `receive()` in `src/server/api.js` (its top comment lists the messages), `applyEvent` / `netHandle` in `src/game/net/client.js`, `chunkSend` / `unchunk` in `src/game/net/transport.js`.

- Client → server: `hello{acct,name,look,save[,user,pass|token]}`, `register{user,pass}`, `logout{token}`, `pos{p:[x,y,z,face,vx,vz]}`, `atk{k:'basic'|'skill'|'burst',tg,face,aim}`,
  `equip{id}`, `unequip{slot}`, `cls{cls}`, `buy/sell{id}`, `merge{id}`, `accept/turnin/abandon{id}`,
  `buyskill{id}`, `eqskill{id[,idx]}` (idx: the passive slot), `unskill{cls,slot[,idx]}` (slot `'pass'` + idx for a passive) (`upskill{id}` is retired: levels come from the Awakening tree, `awk` below),
  `look{look}` (the server keeps `look.aura` / `look.cape` only if you own them: `cosmake`), `cosmake{type:'a'|'c',id}` (version 3: make an aura or a cape at a lodge; `server/cosmetics.js`), `gembuy{v: outfit id, b?: 1}` (spend 1000 gems on an outfit set, or with `b` 1600 on the bundle of the outfit and its whole skill set: `server/amber.js`), `colbuy{i}` (spend 500 gems on premium colour i, 0..47, for the whole account: `server/colours.js`), `colfav{l}` (save the favourite colours, at most 16 numbers), `adwatch{}` (placeholder ad: +15 gems, 3 a day), `gemtest{}` (public testing tool: +1000 gems, no dev switch), `dip{a:'open'|'up'|'go'|'lure',id,k?}` (`lure`: k = a kind id of that pipe, or '' for none; saved as `gear.dip.lure[site id]`; a lured release has half its spawns of that kind and costs x1.5; info adds `lure`, `lureCost`, `lureCosts`) (Dark Infection Pipes: `server/dip.js`, answers `{t:'dip',a:'info',...}`; `{t:'dipa',a:[[id,secs]]}` lists the active pipes), `stuck{}` (also the chat line `/stuck`: sent to the nearest village you may enter, 60 s cooldown; `server/unstuck.js`), `chat{text}`, `name{name}`, `warp{to:'home'|'hanami'|'rimehold'|'highmark'..., any?:1}` (from a teleport circle; the client's travel window; with `any` 1 from anywhere, the map's Travel button: refused in a run, on the Proving Isle, on a cave floor and within 5 s of a hit, `WARP_IDLE`), `mq{a:'talk'|'pick'|'read',id|i}`, `sq{a:'talk',id | a:'track',c}` (side quests: a talk to a villager, the chain the quest log follows; `server/side-quests.js`) (the main quest), `learn{id}`, `gather{i}` (starts a cast) and `sellres{id,n}` (professions), `craft{slot,tier,rar}`, `brew{id,n}` and `potion{k}` (crafting, brewing, drinking), `zt{land:'home'|'vale'|'hoar',n}` (play a land at zone tier n: in a village only), `dev{cmd,v}`.
  Rifts (`server/rift.js`, `docs/RIFT.md`): `rift{a:'open'}` at a tear → `{t:'rift',a:'info',id,e,full,L,best,clears,lv,cost,mode,tiers,tier}` (tiers: the levels an elemental rift offers, tier: the highest cleared); `rift{a:'go',x2:0|1,lv}` starts a solo run (lv: the level picked, elemental rifts only) (the dungeons' `tp` with `dg.m` `'rift'`, the `dg` HUD tuple, `dge` at the end, plus the event `riftw [pid, wave]`); leaving is `dg{a:'leave'}`.
- Server → client: `welcome{...,look?}` (`look` only for a logged-in account: its own look replaces the browser's), `mons{list}` (roster), `you{...}` (private
  state incl. `gear`; `pv`: 1 while you stand on the Proving Isle, where `maxHp` / `dmg` / `def` are the fixed ones), `tp`, `kicked`, `auth{user,token}`, `authfail{text}`,
  `snap{day, n, pl, mo, b (one entry per boss), w (weather), ev:[events]}` 8-20×/s, **made per player**: `mo` holds only the monsters
  you can see (within 110 m, bosses 190 m: the client draws 95 / 170 m) and only when changed since the last time they were sent to
  you (those within 40 m every snapshot, farther ones every second), `pl` yourself and the players within 250 m (the rest once a
  second), `n` the head count. **`b` (the bosses' state, ~170 bytes) is not in every snapshot**: it goes to a player when it differs from what they were last sent, when a boss comes into their range, and with every full resend (3 s); a player in a dungeon run gets the run's boss row in every snapshot (`dgSnapS`) and the world's again when they come back. The client keeps the last rows (`applyBossState`) and re-picks the nearest boss at every snapshot (`pickBoss`). The claude.ai room host still sends it every time. Constants `SNAP_*` and the reasoning are in `server/api.js` (`broadcastSnap`). The claude.ai room
  host keeps one message for everyone (`io.broadcastSnaps`), since its channel is one shared 4 KB topic.
- Events (`ev(...)` on the server, `applyEvent` in `net/client.js`): dmg (`[id,v,crit,by[,fx]]`: fx 1 / -1 = the element helped / hurt), kill, imm, mact, aggro, respawn,
  spawn, despawn, proj, pend, tele (`[id,kind,x,z,r,dur,face,half]`), tend (`[id,fired,x,z]`), zone / zend, wall / wend, pfx (`[pid,kind,dur,vx,vz]`: root, slow, push; the client applies it to itself), roar, area, aend, chain, buff, xp, coins, loot, lvup, hurt, down,
  up, toast, qdone, qturn, pvp (`[pid,1 stepped onto the Proving Isle|0 left it]`), pvlock (`[pid,seconds]`: its gate is shut after a knock-out), pact, rdst (`[raid index, state 0 ready / 1 engaged / 2 cleared, tier, boss slot, stones left, banners left, garrison left, seconds to the rebuild, boss awake]`: a stronghold's state, on every change and one per raid on join), rdlv (`[level, ids]`: the first hit locked a raid's tier; every listed monster's numbers), pjoin, pleave, pgear, plook, pname, chat, merge, skillslot, skillbuy,
  weather, thunder, lvset, warp, vale, north (`[pid,1 ice wall open|2 walked into Rimehold]`), west (`[pid,1 the Greyspine's ice fall open|2 walked into Highmark]`), gate (`[pid,'river'|'neck'|'redgate']`: a rock fall gave way: the Greyspine's west wall, the River Pass, Redgate Canyon), sun (`[pid,2]`: walked into Glasswell), node (`[i,1 taken|0 back]`, a resource node), cast (`[pid,i,seconds]`: your gather cast began) / castx (`[pid]`: it broke), gather (`[pid,i,resource,n]`: the cast ended), drop (`[pid,mat,n,monId]`), skillup, mq (`[pid,stepId,1 started|2 handed in]`), sq (`[pid,chain,step,1 started|2 handed in]`), sqsay (`[pid,place,lines]`: a side quest place's scene for the speech bubble), and for the skill sets `ast` (`[pid,kind,v,dur,piece]`), `fxa` (`[pid,'heal'|'shield',piece]`), `cdr` (`[pid,slot,seconds]`: a cooldown refund) and a last field `piece` on `area` / `proj` / `chain` / `beam`: the set piece that caused it, so the client draws it in the set's look (`docs/skillkits/VISUALS.md`). Monster statuses and skill sets: st (`[monId,kind,v,dur,by]`: `vuln` / `weak`, `server/mon-status.js`), mk (`[monId,markId,n,dur,ownerId]`: a kit's mark, drawn only for its owner, n 0 = spent), proc (`[pid,rowId,monId|0]`: a triggered set bonus with a chance, a cooldown or a streak fired: its badge, a ring and a streak of motes to the monster, `game/skillsets/procs.js`), ast (`[pid,kind,v,dur]`: an ally buff), ssget (`[pid,setId,pos]`: a piece earned); `dev{cmd:'set',v:'<set id>'}` gives a whole set.
- To add a feature that changes state: handle a message in `receive()` (server/api.js), mutate state,
  call `ev('name', ...)` and/or set `p.dirty=true` (→ a `you` update + save), then handle the event in
  `applyEvent` on the client.

## Messages that feature files register (`MSG`, `server/state.js`)

`receive()` falls back to `MSG[msg.t]`, so a feature adds a message without editing the switch: `MSG.party=(p,msg)=>...` (`server/party.js`: `a` = invite / accept / decline / kick / leave / lead...), `MSG.dg` (`server/dungeons/lobby.js`: `a` = open / start / accept / decline / leave / revive / use), `MSG.temper{id,worn}` (`server/dungeon-gear.js`: tempering), `MSG.rwdev` (the testing tool for the rewards, `S.dev` only). Their answers are messages and snapshot fields of their own (`pty`, `dgboard`, `dgi`, `dg` in the snapshot: `server/dungeons/`, `game/dungeon/`; `docs/areas/dungeons.md`). `MSG.fr{a:'add'|'accept'|'decline'|'remove'|'list'|'rank',name}` (`server/friends.js`: the friend list and the ranking; answers are the events `frl` `[pid, rows]` your friends with online state and level, `fri` `[pid, fromName, secs]` a friend request, `rnk` `[pid, rows, myRank, total]` the top 50 by level; the list is saved as `gear.fr`; the panel is `game/ui/friends.js`, comma key). `MSG.pshop{a:'mine'|'add'|'unlist'|'open'|'close'|'browse'|'view'|'buy',id,price,i,name}` (`server/pshop.js`: player shops, registered level 11+, equipment only, coins; answers are the events `pshm` `[pid, open, items]` your stall, `pshl` `[pid, rows]` open stalls, `pshv` `[pid, name, items|null]` one stall; listed items are escrowed in `gear.psh`; panel `game/economy/pshop.js`). `grep -rn "MSG\." src/server` lists them all.

## The Deep Cave's messages (`docs/CAVE.md`)

- Client → server: `awk{a:'tier',set}` / `awk{a:'up',set,id}` / `awk{a:'stat',set,id}` (skills revamp step 9, `server/awakening.js`, `MSG.awk`): `set` is a skill set id or `'wanderer'`, `id` a node id of that character's tree shape (`shared/awakening-tree.js`). `tier` buys the next Awakening tier (A1-A5: the hiker level of `AWK_TIERS`, `awkTierCost`); `up` buys an upgrade node (`ba2`..`bu10`, the unlock nodes `pa1` / `ch1` / `bu1`) or a special burst node (`star1`, `star2`); `stat` a stat node (`sba2`...). A node needs its tier, the tier's hiker level, every `req` node (parent, joins) and not to be owned; a set needs one of its pieces owned. The price (`awkNodeCost`) is charged all at once or not at all: coins, the land's monster drops (`gear.mats`), Echo Shards (`gear.awm.echo`), raid material (`gear.rd.mat`); a trophy or Essence price refuses the purchase until those exist. A refusal is a `toast`. Saved as `gear.awk = {<set|'wanderer'>: {t, n:{<node id>:1}}}`; the levels it gives are written into `gear.skills.lv` / `gear.cave.own`, the played character's stat nodes into `gear.skills.aw`.
- Server → client: `awk` event `[pid, set, 't' | node id, tier]` (a tier or a node was bought; the `you` message that follows carries the new `gear.awk`), and `caveget [pid, skill id]` when `ch1` made a charge skill yours.
- Client → server: `bd{a:'buy',set}` (skills revamp step 12, `server/bond.js`, `MSG.bd` → `bdBuyP`): buys the next Bond step of the character `set` (a skill set id; dev sets and unknown ids are refused). Bond 0..VI cost 6 / 3 / 3 / 6 / 6 / 9 / 9 of that character's Bondstones (`BD_COST`, `shared/bond.js`); Bond I-II ask the set's own zone tier in its land, III-IV tier +1, V-VI tier +2 (at most V), reached = `gear.zt[land].max`. Bond 0 makes the character yours (its basic, skill and power, every class version, go into `gear.skills.owned`; Awakening needs it). A refusal is a `toast`. Saved as `gear.bd = {<set>: {b: -1..6, st: stones}, wanderer: {r: Realization 0..6}}`; what the active Bond gives (min(Bond, the main character's pieces worn) less one per guest piece) is in `gear.skills.bdx = {main, act, g, p, r, lv}` of the `you` message (the Bond passives, the rows, the level changes).
- Server → client: `bd` event `[pid, set, bond]` (a Bond step was bought; the `you` message that follows carries the new `gear.bd`). Stones beyond Bond VI are paid out as coins (`BD_STONE_COINS` = 300 each). The client (step 16, `game/bond/panel.js` `bdcOnBond`) rings the card in the Bonds panel (skills panel and Awakening panel buttons) and redraws it from the `you` that follows.
- Client → server (step 13, `server/bond-orbs.js`, the same `MSG.bd`): `bd{a:'orb',set,n}` buys `n` Bond Orbs of the character `set` at the orb stall (the NPC Orla at the home village's well, panel `game/bond/orbs.js`) for `BD_ORB_PRICE` = 1 coin each: `n` is clamped to 1-99 (`BD_ORB_BUY_MAX`), charged once, never beyond `BD_ORB_MAX` = 999 held of one character; `bd{a:'pick',set}` sets the pouch's pick (`gear.bd.pick`: the character whose orbs open next; `set:null` clears it; if none of that character's orbs are held the set with the most orbs opens). Orbs held are `gear.bd.<set>.o`; refusals are toasts. Opening: `bdOpenOrbP` (1 / 2 / 3 Bondstones at 60 / 30 / 10 %, excess stones as coins).
- Client → server (`server/bond.js` `bdPlayP`, the same `MSG.bd`): `bd{a:'play',set}` picks the character in play (and puts that class's carried weapon in hand: `gear.eq.wpn`, docs/BOND.md section 2; `bd{a:'path'}` does the same for the Wanderer's path class; `equip` of a weapon stores it in its class's slot, and it is in hand only when it is the played class's) (`gear.bd.play`: an owned set id or `'wanderer'`; `set:null` clears it, and the old rule returns: the worn Power piece's set, else the set with the most pieces worn). Free, at any time; the active Bond (`gear.skills.bdx.main`) follows it, the other characters' worn pieces are guests. An unknown or unowned set is dropped. The Bonds panel's Play buttons send it.
- Client → server (step 18, `server/wanderer.js` `wdPathP`, the same `MSG.bd`): `bd{a:'path',id}` changes the Wanderer's active path (`gear.bd.wanderer.path`; `id` a key of `WD_PATHS`, `shared/wanderer.js`: the nine paths; default `fury`). Free and at any time; the Wanderer's tree (`gear.awk.wanderer`) and Realization (`gear.bd.wanderer.r`) stay; a worn path passive (`'wd_<path>'` in `gear.skills.pass`) and a worn path burst (`gear.skills.ult = 'wd_<path>'`) are swapped for the new path's. An unknown id is dropped without a toast. No event: the `you` message that follows carries `gear.bd.wanderer.path`, `gear.skills.owned` (the path passive is owned while the Wanderer's `pa1` is bought) and `gear.skills.wdu` (the path burst `ultequip` accepts: `'wd_<path>'` while `bu1` is bought, else null; derived, never read from a save). The Bonds panel's Wanderer card has the picker (`game/bond/panel.js` `bdcSetPath`).
- Server → client (step 13): `orb` event `[pid, set, stones, coins]` (one of the pid's orbs opened: the stones rolled, coins paid for those beyond Bond VI; the client shows a stacked pop-up, `game/bond/popup.js`, in place of a toast).
- Server → client (step 14, `server/bond-stir.js`): `stir` event `[msg]` and `welcome.stir`, `msg={h, z:{land:zone key}, b:boss id, k:'dg'|'rd', id}`: the hour's Stirrings (`h` = UTC hour number, `BD_STIR_SECS` 3600): one ordinary zone a land, one world boss, one dungeon theme (`k:'dg'`, even hours) or raid (`k:'rd'`, odd hours). Kills in a stirring zone charge `gear.bd.chg` (1, an elite 3; every `BD_STIR_CHARGE` = 60 opens an orb; Pipe, dungeon and raid monsters never count); the client glows the land's stirring zone on the land map and the minimap, rings the boss on the world map and the dungeon / raid on the Delve board and the maps (`game/bond/stir.js`, a box in the Bonds panel lists them); the stirring boss opens `BD_STIR_BOSS[tier]` orbs per helper, a stirring dungeon win 2, a stirring raid clear 3 (each opening sends `orb`).
- Client → server: `atk{k:'cave',tg,face,aim}` casts the worn cave skill (server/cave.js `caveCastP`: level 23, charge >= its cost; the cost is spent, 0.8 s between two casts; the other fields as for any `atk`); (`caveup{id}` is retired: a charge skill's level is the charge branch of the Awakening tree; Echo Shards are `gear.awm.echo`); `cavequip{id}` wears an owned cave skill (`id` null takes it off; registered as `MSG.cavequip`).
- Client → server: `atk{k:'ult',tg,face,aim}` casts the worn set burst (the 5th slot, `server/ult.js` `ultCastP`: level 35, not on the Proving Isle, a fixed 60 s cooldown, 30 s for a grind set outside a boss fight); `ultequip{id}` wears an owned burst by its set id (`id` null takes it off; `MSG.ultequip`). Events: `ult [pid, setId, cooldown]` (a cast), `ultget [pid, setId]` (the burst became owned), `ubo [pid, pieceIds, secs]` (boosted pieces; the message `ubo{ids}` lists those still boosted when one ends); `pact` carries a 5th field `1` for a boosted cast. Rules: `docs/ULT.md`.
- Client → server: `ssall{set}` wears every owned piece of a skill set at once: its three skills, its passives (first open passive slots), its charge (cave) skill and its burst (`server/skillsets.js` `ssEquipAllP`, `MSG.ssall`; the "Equip the whole set" button on a set's card in the skills panel). Pieces that are not owned or are below their level are skipped; one toast says how many were worn.
- Client → server: `lift{to}` (server/cave-zone.js `caveLiftP`, `MSG.lift`): `to` is `0` (the surface lift beside the cave mouth) or a floor number (a number only, anything else is dropped). You must stand on the lift you are at (its radius + 1.5 m), a lift cools down for 2 s, and `caveLiftRefusal` (shared/cave-map.js) decides: below level 23 *"The lift cage will not move..."*, a floor not yet opened *"Floor n is still shut: own 2 skills from floor n-1 first."* (a toast `bad`). The answer is an event `warp` (the flash at both ends), a `tp{x,z,face}` to the arrival 3.4 m in front of the other lift, and a toast; monsters that were after you drop it. There is no "enter the cave" message: a floor is a plain world position (shared/cave-map.js `caveFloorAt`), the client builds the floor's view when a `tp` lands on one (game/cave/zone.js `caveOnTp`). Testing (dev only): `dev{cmd:'tunnel',v:'cave:mouth' | 'cave:2' | 'cave:2,x,z[,deg]'}` (`caveDevTp`).
- Server → client: `cave{c}` is your charge (0-100, rounded), sent when the rounded value changes and once after joining; charge is not saved, so it is not in `you`. A cave cast plays as an ordinary `pact` event (`kind` `cv_<skill id>` unless the skill names its own). The owned skills are `gear.cave.own` ({id: level}) and the worn one `gear.skills.cave`, both in `you.gear`.
- Server → client: `cm{f,hud,cd,a,b,c,d,e,fv,g,h}` (C2, server/cave/methods.js `caveMSendP`) is your state in the cave's eight methods, sent at most 5 times a second and only when it changed, while you stand on a floor with the slot open (level 23); `cm{f:0}` once when you leave the floors. `f` = floor; `hud` = the one line the HUD shows (a sentence, may be empty); `cd` = `{method letter: seconds left}` of your 10-minute cooldowns on this floor (absent when none); `a` = mural pieces you hold (bit mask 0-31); `b` = the three geodes (0 sealed, 1 cracked, 2 charged by the first of a chain); `c` = `{s 0/1 trial running, k killed, n pack size}`; `d` = `{s 0/1 carrying the glow crystal, h hits taken}`; `e` = `{s 0/1 inside the silent chamber, z noise 0-100, w 1 for two seconds after it woke}`; `fv` = `{o seconds the vault stays open (0 = shut), w seconds to the next tremor while it is announced (0 = not announced)}`; `g` = id of the live gem mole (a monster in the roster) or 0; `h` = `{vm, hm veins and herbs you took (bit masks), o ore, b herbs gathered, c 1 while a gather runs}`. Nothing is sent to the server: every method works by where you stand and what you hit. The methods' toasts are ordinary `toast` events; a skill learned is the event `caveget [pid, skill id]` (and `gear.cave.own` in `you`). `gear.cave` also holds `cd` (`{'<floor><method>': epoch seconds}`), `done` (`{'<floor><method>': completions}`) and `sh` (Echo Shards).
- Server → client (events): Second Wind sends `pfx [pid,'clear']`, which clears your slows and roots. The first floor's cave skills draw a placeholder through the `vis` key of their fx (client only).

## Rules every message obeys

- **Sizes.** A WebSocket message is at most 1 MB (the Node host cuts the socket); in the claude.ai room (Shared mode) at most 4 KB, so bigger ones travel as parts in both directions (`chunkSend`, `docs/areas/accounts-net.md`).
- **Rate.** One socket may send 120 messages a second (burst 240); the rest are dropped (`MSG_RATE` in `node/main.js`).
- **Strings are not keys.** `receive()` drops a message that carries the name of an `Object.prototype` member (`constructor`, `__proto__`...) in any field but the free-text ones (`text`, `name`, `user`, `pass`, `token`): the registries are plain objects.
- **A hello's nested JSON is cleaned at the door** (`cleanJsonS`, `server/api.js`): own `__proto__` / `constructor` / `toString` / `valueOf` keys are removed from the whole message, depth is capped at 12 and objects at 20,000 (JSON.parse makes an own `__proto__`; copied into another object it changes that object's prototype, and an own `toString` makes `parseInt(x)` / `obj[x]` throw).
- **Looks are cut down** to flat primitives on arrival (`cleanLookS`, `server/players.js`), saves go through `sanitizeGear`, numbers are clamped (`clampInt`).
- **A handler that throws** is logged by the host and does not stop the world; `S.ctx` (the dungeon run a message belongs to) is reset in a `finally`.
- **Test.** `server-smoke` (a check per feature), `npm run fuzz` (garbage at every handler, mutated saves), `room-smoke` (parts).
