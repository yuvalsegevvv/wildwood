## Pitfalls

- The Greyspine's terrain (the first step of the fourth land taught these): **two lands that meet along a crest must share the crest.** The east wall is the Reach's west wall seen from
  the other side: `greyspineHeight` blends its ground into `hoarBase(x,z)` as the wall rises and then adds the very same terms as `hoarHeight` (`re`, `crest`), and the same shore (`shore` with the same `wide`, so the seam is still under half a metre on the northern coast, `greyspine-smoke` compares it with `valeHeight`); the north has no crest any more: the spine lowers (`amp`) and `shore` brings it down to the northern sea (`shared/coasts.js`), a crest running into the sea (the west wall, the Reach's wall) goes under it over its last 64 m, the Queen's cone is held;
  do the same for any wall two lands share, and put flat places (a village bench, a boss crown) **after** the wall terms or the wall noise roughens them. The south side is not a wall of
  its own: the home forest's rim already saturates into a crest north of HZ0, `baseHeight` just lerps it into the Greyspine over 66 m, which leaves the home forest's heights untouched (the smoke
  test compares `baseHeight` with `homeHeight` / `valeHeight`, and a before/after dump of `rawHeight` showed changes only at z <= -446). `worldBounds` decides which side of the rim you are on from the
  z you had **before** the move (`oz`), so a testing teleport into the Greyspine is not pulled back; nothing in the game crosses the rim on foot yet. A land with no zones must make `zoneAt`
  return null (otherwise the home forest's edge zones, `z - HZ0 < 100`, label it "Greyspine Foothills" and the Sunwall's foot) and `zoneRidge` 0. Mountains: a **valley mask** (`smoothstep(out,in,distance to the
  centre line)`) lerps the troughs' flat floor into the mountains, and the relief is scaled by the distance to the nearest trough (`far`), so the flanks stay walkable while the peaks far from a trough stay high.
  Nothing finer than ~16 m wavelength goes in (the server's grid is 4 m); the whole land costs about 0.3 s of JS to sample at 2 m. **Seeing it for real**: Playwright's Chromium works headless (`--use-gl=angle --use-angle=swiftshader
  --enable-unsafe-swiftshader`, a context with `hasTouch:true` so the game takes its phone path; desktop mode never finishes a frame in software GL), with the `window.__dbg` hook above; one screenshot takes
  a minute or two (give it a 300 s timeout), set `P.x / P.z` for the plants to grow and `window.__cam` for the camera. A hillshade drawn from `rawHeight` (a node script writing a PNG with zlib, no libraries)
  is the quick loop for the shape.

- The rest of the Greyspine (ice fall, Highmark, zones, bosses, water, rock falls) taught these. **A gate through a wall is a canyon, not a door**: `glenCarve` / `westCarve` lerp the ground to the floor height `h` over a ramp and leave the walls beside it standing, and a rubble / ice mesh sinks when the flag is set; the server's `setPos` and the client's `worldBounds` keep you on the right side while it is shut (both must agree, and the testing teleports must set the flags that the clamps look at: `dev tunnel` needs `vale 2` / `north 2` / `west 2` first, the buttons send them). Two features that carve the same ground (Highmark's bench and the glen) must not overlap: measure the bench's spread after moving either. **Water at altitude**: the sea is one plane at y = 0; a tarn, the river and the fjord each get a mesh of their own at their own height with the same material, and everything else asks one function, `waterSurf(x,z)` (0 where there is none): wading and the camera, plants, camp placement (`ok()` rejects `greyWet`), ground colours (wet banks) and the map all read it, so a point you pick for a test can turn out to sit in the river (the ground-colour check in `client-smoke` did). **A new land touches generic code**: the professions' node planner matched every zone with `zn.vale` (49 stray nodes: it now skips `zn.grey`), `MAX_ZONE_LV`, `landAt` / the tiers' `by{}` tables, the bosses' count in three smoke tests, the map's `nextLand`, the circle's travel grid. **A boss's signature is a telegraph kind**: `boss-smoke` demands that each boss have moves no other has, counted by tele / zone / summon kinds (a shared `mode:3` made the Rootwarden's roots test fail), so a new kit gets kinds of its own (rake, swoop, rockfall, quake) and a look for them in `addTeleS` / `endTele`. **Do not dress a model in another family's body**: a harpy built on the wyrm model looked like a wyrm; the gryphon has its own family (`monster-gryphon.js`, wings raised at 0.7 rad and spread when `mode` is 1) and the harpy became a crag wyvern.
