### The borders between the four lands, the Greyfall River and the Greyfall (a waterfall off the home forest's north rim)

`borderX(z)` / `borderXN(z)` / `borderZ(x)` and the helpers `pinK`, `bordNoise`, `wallW` / `wallK` / `wallP` / `wallAdd` (the mountain range's profile), `riverK` / `riverCut` / `riverHalfW` (the river), `FALL` / `fallProfile` / `fallY` / `fallCut` (the waterfall) in `shared/terrain.js`; the land tests `inVale(x,z)`, `inHoar(x,z)`, `inGrey(x,z)`, `landAt(x,z)`, `vilAt(x,z)`; the drawing `game/village/buildings-greyfall.js` (`buildGreyfall`, `updateGreyfall`; the two hooks `// greyfall:` in `generation-setup.js` and `main/loop.js`); the blocking `worldBounds` / `glenWall` / `frostWall` / `greyWall` in `game/player/movement.js` and `northBarZ` (`shared/hoarfrost.js`) with `setPos` (`server/api.js`); test `node tools/greyfall-smoke.js`.

- **The borders are curves, not the rectangle's sides.** The Vale Wall (home forest | vale, south of the junction at z -440; north of it `borderX` is just `HALF`, the stub that ends in the north wall) is the line `x = borderX(z)`, the Greyspine | Reach wall `x = borderXN(z)` (mean `GXJ` = x -140, pinned straight at the glacier valley z -722) and the north wall (home | Greyspine west of x -140, home | Reach between -140 and the junction, vale | Reach east of it) the line `z = borderZ(x)` (pins at x `GXJ`, `HALF` and Frostgate Pass 636). `borderXat(x,z)` is the wall that holds x on its side of the crest. They bend by what each land can spare: the home forest has room, so the river bulges up to 215 m west into it (and 30 m east), the north wall up to 90 m south into it; the vale and the Greyspine own content close to the lines (zones at x 570 and z -300, Highmark, the Sink), so they give at most 55 m (the Reach up to 110 m north of the vale); the Greyspine never loses ground (nothing leans north or west into it). Where something is built across a line it is **pinned straight** (`BORDER_X_PINS`, `BORDER_Z_PINS`: the bridge z -100, the junction z -440 and x 440 (the Greyfall is born there, z -536..-344), the glacier valley z -722, Frostgate Pass x 636), so the tunnel, Hanami, the pass, the glen, Rimehold and Highmark did not move. A new gate on a border needs a pin first; a new content cluster near a line needs the lean checked (`tools/greyfall-smoke.js` states the limits).
- **The land tests take both coordinates.** `inVale(x)` and `x > HALF` are gone: a point is east of the Vale Wall when `x > borderX(z)`, north of the north wall when `z < borderZ(x)`. Everything that decided a land by `HALF` / `HZ0` (zones, ridges, villages, colours, weather, music, the map's names and edges, the camps' `ok()`, respawn, the gates' server clamps, chamois, `terrain-color.js`) asks the lines now; `HALF` and `HZ0` are only the junction and the lines' mean.
- **The mountain range varies.** Both lands on either side of a crest add the same `wallAdd` terms (distance to the crest line, so they meet without a step): a body 58-128 m wide (`wallW`), a crest at x0.55-x1.5 of its height (`wallK`: saddles and massifs), a profile from a long ramp to a steep wall (`wallP`), low ridges that run out into the land (the foothills), none where `riverK` is 1.
- **The Vale Wall south of the junction is a river all the way** (`riverK`: 1 from 90 m south of the junction, ramping in over 70 m, 0 north of it; there is no mountain spur any more): a channel 32-60 m wide and 2.8 m deep at its middle (the sea's level fills it: no new water surface), a low flat flood plain either side (`riverCut`, applied by `homeHeight` and `valeHeight` alike). Nobody crosses it but by **the bridge** (`TUN`, `findTunnel` in `shared/vale.js`: a 4 m wide stone deck from bank to bank at z -100, `bridgeDeck` in `shared/roads.js` counts it as ground, the East Road and the Tunnel Road end at its two ends; `buildBridge` in `buildings-vale.js` draws deck, parapets, piers, a gatehouse with the barred door at the west end and a torii at the east): the river is deeper than the 0.8 m a hiker may wade (the shore's rule, applied in `worldBounds` where `riverK > 0.15` and no deck is under you). The names `TUN`, `inTun`, `findTunnel`, `inTunnelCut` stay from the tunnel the bridge replaced (and the dev command `tunnel` is the teleport). The edge zones treat the river and its bank as the wall's body (`edgeZoneAt`, 56 m), a vale zone reaches 190 m from its seed at most (`valeZoneAt`: the strip the river gives the vale has no zone), the map names it **The Greyfall River** (`edgeName`) and widens the lands' crops (`LANDS`).
- **The Greyfall**: the river is born at the foot of the home forest's north rim, at the junction. On the rim's top (about 140 m up) a tarn (`FALL.tl`; `waterSurf` returns its level) overflows through a slot cut across the crest (the chute, `fallCut`, 6 m half-wide) and falls about 125 m down the rim's face to a plunge pool (`FALL.pool`, 3.4 m deep). `fallProfile` is the fall's path (every 2 m, never rising, never above the ground; computed on first use from `baseHeightRaw`, so no load order problem). `baseHeight` = `baseHeightRaw` + `fallCut` in the fall's box. The client draws a streaked sheet over the path (a canvas texture that runs down it), a foam ring and a puff of spray at the pool (counts by device, nothing updated beyond 340 m) and a two-loop roar (audible from about 420 m, none in a dungeon). Seeing it: `shot.js`-style Playwright views (docs/areas/greyspine.md) at (441, -330) looking north.
- **The north is fitted to the world map** (`GDX` = -580, `RDX` = -390, `GXJ` in `shared/terrain.js`; the story is in `docs/WORLD.md` section 8). The Greyspine was built over x -440..440; it was moved 580 m west **as one rigid piece** (ground, Highmark, zones, Queen, glen, gates, tarns, river, fjord, the Blackseam's door): its noise is sampled in the old frame (`gnx = x - GDX`) so every height tuned on it is unchanged; the Reach's own places (Rimehold, zones h22-h30, two arenas, `FROST_LAKES`, the barrow door) moved `RDX` = 390 m west. **`baseHeightRaw` is split by the north wall's crest**: south of it the home forest / vale as before; north of it the Greyspine west of `borderXN`, the Reach (`valeHeight` into `hoarHeight`) east of it, and the forest's strip between x -140 and 440 north of the rim blends `homeHeight` into `valeHeight` over 66 m. Two terms of `valeHeight` (the Vale Wall's and the north wall's) are weighted by `smoothstep` of z against the crest, so the Reach's south is the north wall seen from the north. Two things to remember: a test that wants "the wall that holds x" must ask `borderXat(x,z)`, and the Greyspine's east wall uses **world-frame** noise (`fbm(x*0.02,z*0.02,3)`, `crest(x,z)`) so it meets the Reach's side without a step (`greyspine-smoke` checks the seam). West of the forest, south of the Greyspine, lies the Sunscar plateau (`inSun`): flat 52 m, not built, a cliff to the sea at `WX0`.
- **Free movement once a land is open; the slope limit.** The border clamps in `worldBounds` act only while the land beyond is locked for you (`valeOpen()`, `northOpen()`, `westOpen()`): the Vale Wall's crest-line clamp, the north wall's (`frostWall`), the glacier valley's (`glenWall`), the home forest's rim above the Greyspine (`greyWall`). Open, nothing but the ground, the water and the slope limit holds you; the barred things that are *objects* stay (the bridge's gate, the ice wall in the pass, the ice fall, the two rock falls, each until its boss falls). The slope limit is `SLOPE_MAX` (1.2 rise over run, about 50 degrees) in `game/player/movement.js`: `slopeBlock` runs after `worldBounds` for a grounded player outside a dungeon and turns an uphill step that is too steep along the slope (20-80 degrees to the flatter side, shorter the more it turns), dropping it when no turn works; going down is free, a jump clears nothing. Test: `tools/slope-smoke.js` (a flood fill from the home village reaches every village, arena, zone, node and door, and 90% of each land's ground) and `client-smoke`.
- **The continent's coast** (`shared/coasts.js`, docs/WORLD.md rule 3: the sea is the world's edge): the vale's south and east, the Reach's north, east and south (east of the vale) and the Greyspine's north are shores, not walls. `coastDist(x,z)` sends every point outside the home forest to `csOuter` (the home forest's own south shore stays as it was: the Tide King's beach depends on it). **The outline**: `CS_BASE` is the designed line, clockwise (x east, z south; each point `[x, z, amp]`: how far noise may bend it there), rounded twice (Chaikin), cut into 30 m pieces and pushed along its outward normal by three octaves of noise (`csBend`: 350, 130 and 50 m), kept as `CS_POLY`; `csDist` measures the distance to it and `csBuild` stores that on a 10 m grid (`CS_SD`, bilinear, 200 m beyond the world) with the share of sea within 110 m (`CS_OPEN`). `csMain` = distance + 21 (the waterline as `shore` reads it) + a wobble of 8 m; `csOuter` adds the islets. **To reshape the coast edit `CS_BASE`** (points: the Greyspine's north coast with its Queen's cape, the step north at the wall, the Reach's north coast with two capes and two deep bays, the east coast's bays and its long cape, no south coast for the Reach (it borders the home forest along its wall; the corner north-east of the vale, 330 m beyond the vale's east edge, was cut), the vale's east coast with its two capes, the bay that took the south-east corner, the vale's south coast; a cape tip should stay 50 m or more inside the rectangle: `CS_POLY` clamps at 35 m) and look at it with a hillshade of `rawHeight`. Keep the amp of a point in step with what is behind it: the vale's zones and nodes lie 50-130 m from its east and south shores, the Reach and Greyspine north have room. **The Warlord Isles** (`CS_ISLANDS`: x, z, radius, east-west stretch): the vale's south-east corner (x > 840, z > 190) is sea and zone 24, Warlord Ruins, moved onto the first island (its seed in `VALE_SEEDS`, `shared/vale.js`: camps and nodes follow because zones and nodes are found from the seed); `The Isle Road` (`shared/roads.js`) runs from the Shrine Road over zones 23 and 25 and an 83 m plank causeway (found automatically where the road runs under water, named by `CAUSEWAY_NAMES`: The Warlord Causeway) to the island; two small islands beside it are only land. A big island keeps its hills (`csWide` is short near it, `valeHeight` lifts its ground to a beach at least, no ponds); its waterline wobbles slowly (`csIslandC`). To move more content to an island: add it to `CS_ISLANDS`, move the zone's seed onto it, give it a road, and `tools/coasts-smoke.js` and `slope-smoke` say whether it is reachable. **The Reach grew**: `EAST_W` = 880 and `NORTH_D` = 840 (shared/terrain.js; the world is 2340 x 1720 m, `WX0` = -1020 to `WX1` = 1320: the west is the Greyspine's far end and the Sunscar plateau; a client heightmap takes about 2.5 s longer to build on a desktop than it did before the Reach grew), `VALE_E` and `GREY_N` are the vale's east and the Greyspine's north edge as they were; the rest of the rectangle is sea. A sea cell returns -5 early (`valeHeight`, `greyspineBase`: `c < -8`, away from the river's mouth and the Greyspine's west wall) so the new sea costs almost nothing; zone ridges stop at the shore (`rawHeight`). `shore(h,c,keep,wide)` brings the ground down to a ~20 m beach (c 22-40) and under the sea (c below ~21); `wide` lengthens the run for high land (the Reach 24 m, the Greyspine 60 m, plus up to 110 m by `csWide` where more than half of the 110 m round is sea, so a bay has a long gentle beach and a cape a bluff). **Holds** (`CS_HOLD`, `csHold`): the vale's boss arena is 110 m from the south coast, so around it the sea keeps 50 m off and `shore` leaves the ground alone; the Gryphon Queen's cone (232 m) is a headland for the same reason (its `keep` in `greyspineBase`). **Islets** (`CS_ISLES`, found at load: a grid of candidate spots in the sea band, thinned by a seeded rng, each kept only if the ring round it is all sea). A node needs `rawHeight >= 30` in the Reach, so a zone that loses ground to a bay may need more tries (`put` in `professions.js` tries 240 times). The beaches are shingle in the Reach and the Greyspine (`COL.shingle`), sand in the vale; the map names the shore (`edgeName`). To see it: the Testing tab's buttons (`tCoastReach`, `tCoastCape`, `tCoastGrey`, `tCoastVale`, `tCoastEast`: beaches next to the capes and bays; they open the ways first), a hillshade of `rawHeight` sampled with `loadShared(['rawHeight'])`, or the real client (Playwright, docs/areas/greyspine.md; keep the camera above the ground: the plateau is 55-60 m up, and the first view of a run often has not streamed in, so make it a dummy); test `node tools/coasts-smoke.js`.
- **Test the geometry, not the look**: `greyfall-smoke` checks the curves (amplitudes, one-sided leans, the pins), that every point is in exactly one land and its village agrees, the river's depth, width and banks all along, the bridge, the fall's path, the tarn's rim and the pool; the client side (the mesh, the clamps, the map's names) is in `client-smoke`.

## Pitfalls

- Adding a region (the Hoarfrost Reach taught these): `WZ0` is the whole world's north edge now, not the forest's or the vale's: their edge is `HZ0`
  (`inHoar(x,z)` tells the Reach apart; `inVale(x)` is true there too). A zone key is a level for the home forest and the vale but `'h22'`.. for the Reach
  (`ZONES.find(z=>z.key===level)` finds only the first two: use `z.level===L`, `defZone(def)`). Hoarfrost zones carry `vale:true` (a polar cell with `x,z,R`)
  and `hoar:true`. `upgradeNeeds` must cap the level it asks drops of (`VALE_TOP_LV`): above it every kind has a `zone` and the pool is empty.
  Constants used by terrain functions at load must be defined before the first call (TDZ across the concatenated files): `FROST_LAKES`, `hoarBase` live in
  `shared/terrain.js` for that reason. A new region also needs `ok()` in `server/monsters.js`, `worldBounds` (movement.js), the map's `LANDS`, `vilAt`, the music and
  ambience, and a look at every `inVale(x)` (vegetation, petals, motes).

  (its circle works). Professions: `gear.prof` `{mining|woodcutting|gathering: {xp}}` (60 coins each at any Lodge), `gear.res` the resources, `gear.pot` the potions; the tools are worn in `gear.eq.pick|axe|sickle`.
