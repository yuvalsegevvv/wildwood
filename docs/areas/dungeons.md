## Where to change what

### Dungeons (**planned, not built**: only the pure setup exists and nothing calls it): the map tiles (24 x 24 cells of 2 m = 48 m; `DG_SET_BARE`, `dgRect`, `dgVariants`; the round hall is a boss arena's circle, `DG_BOSS_R` = 20 m = every `ARENAS` r), the seeded layout generator (`dgLayout`, `DG_MISSIONS`: the roles each mission needs, one boss hall each: every mission ends with a boss, no extraction), the baked grid both sides share (`dgBake`, with `boss` = the hall's circle as an arena `{x,z,r}`; `dgSolid` / `dgFree` / `dgSlide`, `dgLos`, the monsters' flow field `dgFlow` / `dgStep`), the three dungeons as data (`DG_THEMES`: land, `at` zone, walkers and guardians, boss, music, palette; `DG_LV` 30), their three new bosses (`DG_BOSSES`: model, pal, moves named by primitive, the `needs` still to build), the hourly offer of two mission types (`dgOffer`, `dgOfferLeft`: random but fair, UTC) and who may enter (`dgUnlocked`, `dgLevel`, `DG_LANDS`: the difficulty is the land's own zone tier setting; Wildwood's is locked at +0 and level 30 at +1, the Vale's and the Reach's level 30 at +0 after Hanami / Rimehold, +10 levels a tier above the base, entry from level - 5), party-size scaling (`DG_PARTY`, `dgParty`, `dgFightRatio`), the three entrances (`DG_ENTRANCES`: a door each on the real terrain, found by scanning it, with a signpost on the nearest road; `dgApron`, `dgEntranceNear`: the board opens only within `DG_ENT_TALK` of a door; `dgGateOpen`: Wildwood's door is shown sealed at +0; a camp keeps 32 m off a door), and the rewards in `shared/dungeon-rewards.js` (a clear pays one random level-30 item: weapons from Wildwood's dungeon, armour from the Vale's, a ring from the Reach's, rarity 70 / 25 / 4 / 0.8 / 0.2%, `DG_REWARDS`, `dgClearReward`; the level-30 gear is a tier above the six, ids `sword7-e+3`, `dgParse` / `dgItem`; the ring, 7 types, adds `ringAtk` of your weapon's attack when its element is your soul's; enhancing up to `ENH_MAX` 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 by rarity with the Tempering Stone, which normal monsters of level 30+ drop at 2.6% instead of equipment, `dgDropKind`)
